Our Story · Since 2004

Two decades of shaping global citizens

Aethris was founded on a simple, radical belief: that a truly great education develops not just brilliant minds, but good and courageous human beings.

Our Mission

To cultivate curious, compassionate and courageous learners.

We deliver a rigorous, globally recognised education inside a nurturing community — empowering every student to discover their gifts, challenge their limits, and contribute meaningfully to the world.

Our Vision

To be the world's most inspiring international school.

A school where innovation meets tradition, where every child is known and valued, and where graduates leave ready to lead lives of purpose across any border they choose to cross.

Global Education Philosophy

Learning without borders or ceilings.

Our philosophy blends the best of international frameworks with a deeply personal approach. We believe education should be experiential, inquiry-driven and joyful — preparing students not for a single exam, but for a lifetime of learning and leadership.

  • Inquiry over memorisation — students learn to ask, investigate and create.
  • Global mindset — 62 nationalities learning empathy and collaboration daily.
  • Whole-child development — academics, arts, athletics and character in balance.
  • Future fluency — technology, sustainability and innovation woven throughout.

Our Legacy

The Founding

Aethris opens its doors with 120 students and a bold vision for international education in Egypt.

2009

2014

The New Campus

Unveils its landmark 42-acre campus with world-class labs, studios and athletic facilities.

2019

Innovation Center

Launches the AI, Robotics and Aviation programs — a first for the region.

2024

20 Years of Excellence

Celebrates two decades with a 98% university acceptance rate and alumni across six continents.
